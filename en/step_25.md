## Tune the obstacle speed

Choose how quickly the obstacles cross the Stage.

Change the starting value of `speed`{:class="block3variables"} in the obstacle's setup script.

A more negative number, such as `-7`, makes every clone move faster. A number closer to zero makes them move more slowly.

## Now run your code

Play the game a few times and keep adjusting `speed`{:class="block3variables"} until the obstacles move at the pace you want.
