## Add a character

The starter project is open beside these instructions, with an empty Stage ready for your game.

Add any character with **Choose a Sprite**.

This example uses `Pico`{:class="block3looks"}, but you can choose how you would like your character to look.

![The Choose a Sprite button in the bottom-right of the Scratch editor.](images/sprite-choose.png)

Your character appears on the Stage. You'll choose where it starts over the next two steps.
