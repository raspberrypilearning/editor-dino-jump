## Add a character

![Pico.](images/Pico-a.png){:width="100px" height="100px" style="object-fit: contain;"}

The starter project is open beside these instructions, with an empty Stage ready for your game.

Click **Choose a Sprite**.

![The Choose a Sprite button in the bottom-right of the Scratch editor.](images/sprite-choose.png)

Find **Pico** in the sprite library and click on it.

## Check your project

**Pico** appears on the Stage. There's no code to run yet.
